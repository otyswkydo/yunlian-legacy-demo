Opera · Calligraphy · Flower Arranging Classes

Experience the beauty of the East — from a brushstroke and a flower to a song and a line of verse. Veteran opera teacher Zhang Xudong (Chair of the Canada Jiuzhou Art Center) teaches Peking Opera in person, introducing this national art through its four skills: singing, recitation, acting and martial arts. We also offer calligraphy and flower-arranging classes, where quiet writing and floral design open a window onto traditional aesthetics and Eastern philosophy. Suitable for culture lovers of all ages.

Chinese & Western Pastry and Dough Art Classes

From Chinese huamo to festive cakes, food can carry emotion and culture. Taught hands-on by Ms. Jade, a third-generation heritage inheritor, these classes cover traditional dough sculpture (longevity peaches, roses, zodiac animals and more) combined with creative Western dessert ideas to make festive treats. Fun for all ages — ideal for families, dessert beginners and lovers of cultural handicrafts.

Festival Heritage DIY Workshops

One class, one blessing, one memory. Around Chinese New Year, Mid-Autumn, Christmas and other traditional and international festivals, we regularly host heritage DIY activities — handmade longevity peaches, zodiac cookies, blessing buns and more — so children and families can create together and discover the warmth and meaning behind each festival. Activities are offered in several languages, and friends from every background are welcome to share in the love and beauty of festive traditions.

Intangible Heritage Summer Camp

Spend a whole summer planting a seed of culture. Designed for young people aged 6 and up, our culture camp covers dough food art, Peking Opera performance, folk crafts, festival stories and more, combining hands-on activities, performance and arts education to spark creativity and cultural identity. With flexible classes and a lively atmosphere, it's the perfect choice for summer growth and multicultural discovery.
